Case 2 — "show me reviews of Game of Thrones and season 1, episode 1 simultaneously." Reviews tile is live web (QtWebEngine). The episode cannot be tiled — Widevine DRM forbids compositing HBO/Netflix/Prime into another surface. So the tile shows episode metadata + trailer, and “play it” hands off fullscreen to the Fire TV via ADB.

The rule the mockup encodes: non-DRM content (recipes, web, YouTube, trailers, your own media) tiles freely on the Genius TV. DRM streaming goes fullscreen on the Fire TV via ADB. The dashboard is the briefing surface; the TV is the playback surface.
